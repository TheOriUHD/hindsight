Clip sharing terms

What sharing does

When you press Share, Hindsight uploads that clip. Anyone with the link can watch it, react to it and download it. Nothing is uploaded unless you press Share.

How long clips stay

Clips are deleted automatically after 14 days. You can remove a clip sooner from the Hindsight app. Limits on size, daily uploads and total storage apply, and sharing can be unavailable when they're reached.

What you can't share

  • Anything illegal, including sexual content involving minors.
  • Content you don't have the right to share.
  • Harassment, threats, hate, or other people's private information.
  • Sexual content, or graphic violence outside of normal gameplay.

Clips that break these rules are removed.

Reporting a clip

Every clip page has a report form. Reports are reviewed, and a clip can be hidden while that happens.

What's stored

The clip, its thumbnail, title and game, and reaction counts. To enforce limits, your IP address is stored only as a salted hash, and upload limit records are deleted within two days. Reports are kept for up to twelve months. The privacy policy has the details.

Contact

Write to contact@hindsighthq.app, in English or German. The operator's details are in the Impressum.
